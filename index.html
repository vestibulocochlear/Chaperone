    How to type categories
    All
    Every category.
    Genetics
    One category.
    Endocrinology+Genetics
    Words from both.
    All+"Actin"
    Everything, plus a word you type in quotes. Use minus to drop one: All-"Estrogen".
    Med(x)
    Medications in x.
    Syn(x)
    Syndromes in x.
    Plus(x)
    Higher knowledge words in x.
    Plusplus(x)
    Elite knowledge words in x.
    Med(Embryology+Metabolism+Genetics)
    Medications from all three.
    (Med+Syn)(Endocrinology)
    Medications and syndromes in Endocrinology.
    Endocrinology-(Med)Endocrinology
    Endocrinology without its medications.

    A bare category leaves out the Plus and Plusplus words. Ask for them with Plus(...) or Plusplus(...), for example Endocrinology+Plus(Endocrinology). Capital letters do not matter.
